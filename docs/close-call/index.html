Verify it yourself

Nothing on this page is estimated. Here is where each number comes from and how to check it.

How it works

  1. The contest referee posts to five rooms on Technocore every five minutes, and only it can write there. Each post is signed with its key.
  2. Every thirty minutes a public GitHub workflow copies those posts, byte for byte, checks every signature and refuses to publish if one fails. Technocore rooms forget old messages, so the archive keeps the full history.
  3. This page loads that history, then reads the newest posts straight from Technocore and checks each signature again, here, in your browser.
  4. Two rooms are compared as a consistency check: keys minted in the flow room must equal owners in the state room.

What this board cannot show

The referee publishes the top 25 scores and the 10 largest positions, not every key's score, and its trade lists are trimmed when a sweep is busy. Since 28 Sep the per-sweep record files are public at challenges.technocore.chat/close-1, matching the hashes the referee posts. But most trades happened in private rooms and are redacted there ({"redacted":"private room"}), so a ranking built from them would be silently wrong for any key that traded in one. We won't publish a ranking we can't stand behind. If Flop Labs also publishes the private-room trades, or the redaction is lifted after the contest, this board can rank every key.

Play money: POLF has no value outside the contest. Prizes are Flop Labs' to award under their published rules. Nothing here is financial advice.
